the geography of hearts—
15th, dec 2025
The ones beside me know my name, my laugh, my passing face they walk with me through crowded days yet never touch the quiet space.

Their voices stay at surface depth, small talk, borrowed light, present in the room I stand in, absent in my inner night.

But those who live a world away, in miles, in time, in fate, sit gently in my beating chest and never feel like late.

They know my pauses, my unsent words, the ache I never show, they read my silences like poems I was too scared to let go.

Distance failed to thin their warmth, or make their presence weak they reach me without a single call, without a chance to speak.

So I've learned that closeness isn't found in footsteps near my door, but in the ones who live in my heart even when they're far, and more.
