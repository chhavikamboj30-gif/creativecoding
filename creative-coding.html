Creative Coding Assignment 1

Meet Momo the Hoodie Monkey
He is a playful little monkey inspired by the warmth and imagination of childhood memories. Designed in p5.js for a Creative Coding assignment, this character combines a simple toy-like appearance with a fun modern twist through its bright yellow hoodie and cheerful expression. The idea behind Momo came from a friend’s childhood description of a comforting and friendly toy companion. The soft shapes, minimal details, and vibrant colors were intentionally used to recreate the innocent and joyful feeling of childhood playtime. This project helped explore how coding and creativity can work together to transform emotions and memories into interactive digital art.

Meet Luna MoodMirror
She is a playful and expressive digital character who loves changing her style with every interaction. With her curious eyes following your every movement, Luna feels almost alive on the screen. She enjoys experimenting with colorful outfits, magical background moods, and different hairstyles that transform with just a click or key press. Created in p5.js as part of a Creative Coding project, Luna represents creativity, emotion, and interaction coming together in a fun and engaging digital experience.
