in this small corner

I turn ordinary weeks into something worth remembering

I am a collection of quiet thoughts and loud dreams, constantly learning how to exist in between the two. I notice small things — the way light falls on ordinary afternoons, the feeling of a song that understands me too well, the poetry hidden inside everyday chaos. I think deeply, feel deeply, and sometimes get lost in my own reflections. I am growing, unfolding, and becoming — slowly, honestly, imperfectly. This space is where my weeks turn into words, where moments are held still for a second longer, and where I try to make sense of the beautiful, complicated experience of being human.
