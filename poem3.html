the art of quiet waiting—
5th, july 2025
Still she listens by the shore, Where his voice returns no more. Once the tides would call him home, Now they break in silent foam.

She waits in dreams the night has spun, Counting days that never come. Footsteps lost, a hollow sound, Echoes buried underground.

Her heart pretends it has no scars, Yet hopes still flicker like the stars. Bones grow wise and learn to sigh, But love too proud to say goodbye.

She does not beg for him to stay, Nor curse the dark or plead with day. Hope does not fade or truly die- It simply learns to weep and lie
